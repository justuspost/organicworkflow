What We Run

Four levers decide whether a trades business makes money.

We don't hand you a playbook and wish you luck. We operate these functions on your behalf, with our own people and our own systems.

01 / PRICING

Jobs priced optimally

Every ticket is built from real labor, material and overhead numbers — then tested against what your market actually pays. No guessing, no leaving money on the counter, no pricing yourself out of the neighborhood.

02 / HIRING

Crews hired optimally

Sourcing, screening, background and license verification, pay banding, and onboarding. You approve who joins your truck. We handle finding them and everything that comes after the handshake.

03 / MARKETING

Demand generated optimally

Search, local maps, paid ads, review generation and repeat-customer campaigns — measured by booked revenue per dollar, not impressions. When the phone is quiet, that is our problem to fix.

04 / CLOSING

Jobs closed optimally

Live call answering, fast quotes, structured follow-up, financing options and good-better-best presentation. Leads stop dying in voicemail and unanswered estimates.

Questions

The things every tradesman asks first.

Is this a franchise?

No. There's no franchise fee, no territory purchase, and no royalty on gross. This is a working partnership: we invest the business infrastructure, you invest the trade expertise, and we agree in writing on how the economics are shared before anything starts.

What does it cost me up front?

No startup capital is required from you. Organic Workflow funds the setup — entity, insurance, phones, marketing, software and initial lead spend. You bring your license, your skills and your tools.

Do I stay in the field or move into an office?

Your call, and it usually changes over time. Most partners start on the truck and step back as their crew grows. Either way, the office work stays with us.

Who owns the customers and the brand?

The business is jointly held under terms we agree on in advance, and every partner gets a written, plain-language agreement covering ownership, customer records, brand rights and what happens if either side walks away. Nothing about that is a surprise later.

Which trades and which areas?

Any licensed or certified residential and light-commercial trade: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, garage doors, appliance repair, concrete, landscaping and more. We're based in Tulsa and we work northeastern Oklahoma only — the Tulsa metro and the surrounding communities. We'd rather own one region properly than spread thin across a map.

I already have my own small company. Still relevant?

Often the best fit. If you're booked but not profitable, or profitable but stuck at one truck, we can take over pricing, marketing, closing and admin and grow what you've already built. Tell us where you are in the form and we'll take it from there.
